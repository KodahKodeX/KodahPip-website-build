Everything is tuning.

A body is an instrument. So is a marriage, a team, a company, a city. Each one is a system of signal and feedback, resonance and dissonance. Each one can drift out of tune. And each one can be brought back.

Language is a tuning technology. So are ceremony, hypnosis, medicine, design and, now, artificial intelligence. They all change the signal a person sends and receives. Some bring us into coherence. Some pull us out of it.

## The self

It starts in the body. When you lose contact with it, every signal you send and receive gets distorted. You misread the room. You say yes to the wrong things. You make decisions from compulsion instead of presence.

I know this because I have been out of tune in ways most people never have to imagine, and I found my way back. The lesson I took from it is simple: tune the body and the life before modifying them.

## Relationships

Families and teams are ensembles. Coherence does not mean everyone plays the same note. It means distinct voices, each fully itself, playing together. Forced agreement is not harmony. It is silence.

## Contribution

When the self and the ensemble are in tune, something changes in the world around them. People find the right rooms: the community, the work and the places their gifts were made for. Dissonance is not a verdict. It is information.

> The challenge is not strategy. The challenge is coherence.

This is the thread through everything I build: the talks, the gatherings, the places, the clothes. Tune the self. Tune relationships. Tune contribution. Then tune everything.
