I've been integrating over a concept of Deep Accelerationism that I see as the alignment of memetic creation and living systems. The key for me has been the observation that I consistently experience environmental frames of our civilization as myopic to solely "Earth as Nature."

<p class="beat" aria-hidden="true">·</p>

When in reality, the entire cosmos is objectively nature; including our cell phones, the asteroids and planets of resources minerals to discover, and the intellectual creations of consciousness --especially those evolutionarily sorted by human selection.

<p class="beat" aria-hidden="true">·</p>

two frameworks positioned in false polarity. There are metabolic and consumption constraints hard coded into the physics and economics of our world. These two camps are more similar than their branding purports.

<p class="beat" aria-hidden="true">·</p>

My observation is that when an ideology makes another aspect of the dialogue wrong in some way, they get something subtle in tandem. This often looks like not having to strengthen a weakness in stance and structure.

<p class="beat" aria-hidden="true">·</p>

This 'deep accelerationism' idea of mine is something along the lines of Techno-naturalism and the longevity of intellectual technology as key orientations for an optimal multi-planetary civilization that operates within, and augments, the carrying constraints of the planetary biospheres involved. And thereby, could be a key to reaching a level one civilization on the Kardashev Scale ([https://futurism.com/the-kardashev-scale-type-i-ii-iii-iv](https://futurism.com/the-kardashev-scale-type-i-ii-iii-iv-v-civilization))

I have more thoughts on this.
