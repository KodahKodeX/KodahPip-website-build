Promt engineer studio behind it allChat GPT3's take on the way forward:

To use AI to develop generative AR (Augmented Reality) game experiences, you can leverage machine learning algorithms such as generative adversarial networks (GANs) and variational autoencoders (VAEs) to create and generate AR assets such as 3D models, textures, animations, and scenes. These algorithms can be trained on existing AR datasets and then be used to generate new AR content that fits within the game environment and enhances the overall player experience. Additionally, AI can be used to generate the game logic and dynamically adjust the game environment based on player actions, providing an even more immersive and personalized experience. Another area where AI can be used is in generating realistic physics simulations for AR objects, making the game experience more believable and engaging. Overall, the use of AI in AR game development can lead to faster and more cost-effective production times, as well as a wider variety of unique AR game experiences for players.
